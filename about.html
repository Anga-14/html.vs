About SchoolHop Foundation

SchoolHop works to bridge the distance between rural learners and their classrooms by supporting safe, reliable transport in the Eastern Cape.

Our mission

We help learners in rural communities reach school consistently, reducing the distance and transport barriers that can stand between children and education.

Our approach

SchoolHop coordinates village transport routes and works with community and funding partners to keep learner journeys safer and more dependable.

Why the work matters

Reliable transport supports school attendance and gives families, educators, and sponsors a practical way to contribute to learners’ education.
