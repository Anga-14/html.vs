Our Product and Services

SchoolHop’s core service is free, safe, and reliable daily transport that helps rural learners across the Eastern Cape travel between their communities and school.

  • Village school routes

    Organized transport routes connect learners in rural communities with their schools.

  • Daily learner transport

    Dependable journeys help learners arrive at school and take part in their education.

  • Route sponsorship

    Organizations and supporters can help fund transport routes for the communities that need them.

  • Community partnerships

    We work with supporters and local communities to strengthen access to education through transport.
